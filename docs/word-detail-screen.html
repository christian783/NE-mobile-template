ephemeral

/ɪˈfɛm.ər.əl/
Adjective

Lasting for a very short time.

"Fashions are ephemeral: new ones regularly drive out the old."

Origin

Late 16th century: from Greek ephēmeros (from epi ‘on’ + hēmera ‘day’) + -al.
