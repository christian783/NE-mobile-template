ephemeral

/ɪˈfem.ər.əl/
Playing...
adjective
1.

Lasting for a very short time; transitory or short-lived.

"Fashions are ephemeral."

2.

(chiefly of plants) Having a very short life cycle.

"Ephemeral plants that emerge briefly in the desert after rain."

Synonyms

transitory fleeting evanescent momentary

Etymology

Late 16th century: from Greek ephēmeros (from epi 'on' + hēmera 'day') + -al.
